FEATURED PROJECT

# Integrated Biogas, Forage and Dairy Circular Economy Project

## Furi, Sheger City, Ethiopia

Africa Climate Actions PLC is advancing an integrated circular agriculture concept that connects livestock production, renewable energy, organic fertilizer, forage production, and clean transport energy.

The project demonstrates how livestock manure can become a productive resource rather than a waste management problem.

## The Circular System

• Livestock manure is collected from dairy and livestock production facilities.

• The manure is processed through a biodigester to produce biogas.

• The biogas can be cleaned and upgraded for productive energy applications, including its potential use as vehicle fuel for public transport.

• The material remaining after digestion is processed and used as organic fertilizer.

• The fertilizer supports forage and feed production.

• The resulting forage and feed return to livestock production, completing the resource cycle.

## From Manure to Energy

Anaerobic digestion converts livestock manure into renewable biogas. Instead of releasing methane from unmanaged manure, the system captures its energy value. Depending on project scale and technical configuration, cleaned biogas can support cooking, heating, electricity generation, industrial energy use, or transport applications.

## From Digestate to Agricultural Production

Biogas production also generates digestate containing plant nutrients and organic matter. With proper treatment and management, this material can be used as an organic fertilizer. The project links digestate recycling with forage production, helping return nutrients to the agricultural system and reducing waste.

## Linking Energy and Food Production

The integrated model connects livestock, manure, energy, fertilizer, forage, and transport. This approach creates several potential benefits:

• Reduced methane emissions from manure

• Renewable energy production

• Improved manure management

• Organic fertilizer production

• Nutrient recycling

• Forage and feed production

• Reduced dependence on fossil energy

• Reduced waste

• New business opportunities

• Potential carbon finance opportunities

## A Model for Circular Agriculture

The project is designed as an example of how cities, livestock producers, transport systems, agricultural enterprises, and climate finance can work together. The model can be adapted for dairy clusters, livestock production areas, municipalities, cooperatives, commercial farms, and agro industrial systems across Africa.
